2048 Puzzle: Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026 · App by RK Solutions

In short: 2048 Puzzle has no accounts and does not collect your personal information itself. Your games and settings stay on your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device data to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Who we are

2048 Puzzle (the "App") is made by RK Solutions ("we", "us"). Contact: rksolutionsoffical@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Your saved games, best scores, statistics and settings are stored only on your device. We do not receive them. They are deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: optional reward videos (for an extra undo or to shuffle the board) and occasional full-screen ads at the end of a game. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • interactions with ads (views, clicks).

This data is collected directly by Google, not by us. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your phone).

Ad-blocker check

Because the App is funded by ads, it checks at start-up whether Google's ad servers can be reached, by looking up their addresses. No information about you is sent to us during this check.

Sharing

If you tap Share, the App creates a picture of your game and opens your phone's share menu. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose an app to share with.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data security and retention

We do not run servers that store your data. Data processed by Google for ads is kept according to Google's policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
